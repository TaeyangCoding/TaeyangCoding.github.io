# TaeyangCoding.github.io
Public app support links and app-ads.txt for Dmoney and My Calculator

## App catalog

The homepage and `/apps/` share the reviewed data in `apps/apps.json`.
Run `python3 tools/build_site.py` after editing it. Both pages work without
JavaScript; JavaScript adds search and platform filters.

Store availability was checked in the Korean storefront on 2026-11-09.
Eight Apple listings were public. Three Apple listings and seven Google Play
listings were not public. Their registered URLs remain in the catalog data,
but download links are shown only when the listing is verified public.
Recheck the official store before changing a listing's status to `public`.
Keep iOS and Android privacy links separate. Do not replace app-specific
policies with a general homepage policy.
